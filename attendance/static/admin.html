🦺 How the ramp safety pack works

    
    
      
  • At clock-in, staff in the departments below confirm their PPE with one tap per item — the hi-vis vest is auto-detected by the camera.
    • 
      
  • Where the fatigue check is on, they also rate how rested they feel (1–5). Aura shows a fatigue-risk score with its reasons: rest since last shift, hours in the last 24 h / 7 days, consecutive days, night work, recent “tired” remarks and the self-rating.
    • 
      
  • Missing PPE or a HIGH fatigue score is flagged to supervisors (Insights, and SAFETY_WEBHOOK_URL if set) — it never blocks clock-in.
    • 
    

    
The fatigue score is a transparent risk indicator to prompt a conversation — not a medical assessment or a certified fatigue risk management system (FRMS).

  

  

    
Department rules

      

    
Departments without a rule clock in as normal. Limits are used for the fatigue score.
