People

Click a person to edit their profile. Aim for 3–5 face photos per person (front, slight left/right, with/without glasses) — use the Enrol tab. Names are what appears on the kiosk.

Mood tracking

With consent, the kiosk reads a mood estimate (on-device, from the face) at each visit. Turning it off stops mood tracking for this person and erases all their past mood readings.
